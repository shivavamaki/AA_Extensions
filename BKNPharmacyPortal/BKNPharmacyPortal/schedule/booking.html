Booking rules
  • Regular pharmacists can request day off up to 5 days/month.
  • Only 1 Saturday and 1 Sunday can be requested per month.
  • HOD is excluded from operational schedule, so booking is not required.
  • Part time books available work days instead of requested day off.
  • Editor / manager can override any booked date and assign a shift during allocation.
