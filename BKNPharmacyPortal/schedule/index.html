Step 1: click cells or use Pharmacist booking page to fill REQ OFF first, then Save to database in server to lock them. Step 2: click Auto Allocate to generate fair shifts. Step 3: admin may manually change shift; Refresh / Regenerate will keep REQ OFF and admin-changed cells.
App workflow
  1. First opening: table is blank for admin to enter REQ OFF and part-time available days, or load bookings from the Pharmacist booking page.
  2. Save to database in server: REQ OFF becomes locked and protected from accidental close/change.
  3. Auto Allocate: system fills reasonable shifts, balances shift burden, and keeps total work hours as equal as possible.
  4. Admin manual change: click a cell after allocation and choose an available shift. Refresh / Regenerate will not touch REQ OFF or admin-changed cells.
